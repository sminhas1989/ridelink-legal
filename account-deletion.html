Delete Your RideLink Account

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Account deletion is self-serve and available directly inside the RideLink app — you don't need to contact anyone to request it.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the RideLink app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning — it shows exactly what deleting your account will remove, including any motorcycles in your Garage, an active ride if one is in progress, and any group ride or event you organize (deleting your account also removes that group ride/event for every other member, since there's no separate leader-transfer feature).
  5. Confirm. The app signs you out automatically once deletion completes.
Deletion is immediate and permanent. Once confirmed, your account and the data below cannot be recovered, and the same email/password can't be used to sign in again.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (email, username, display name, bio, avatar)
  • Every motorcycle in your Garage, and their photos
  • Your ride history, including saved routes and statistics
  • Your live location (already temporary by design — see the Privacy Policy)
  • Your friendships, in both directions
  • Any group ride or event you organize — for every member, not just you

Can't open the app?

If you no longer have access to a device with RideLink installed, email sminhas1989@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll process the deletion manually.
