RideLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

RideLink is currently in private beta. This policy is a good-faith, accurate description of what the app does today, drafted to reflect its actual behavior rather than generic boilerplate — but it has not yet been reviewed by legal counsel. It will be finalized before RideLink is generally available.

1. Introduction

RideLink ("we," "our," "the app") is a mobile application for motorcycle riders to see when friends are riding, organize and join group rides, share live location safely during active rides, and keep a personal history of their rides. This policy explains what information RideLink collects, how it's used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information you provide directly: email address, username, display name, bio, and avatar image.
  • Garage information: motorcycles you add to your profile (make, model, year, and an optional photo).
  • Ride data: duration, distance, and — only if you choose to set one — a destination, for each ride you record. A simplified version of your route may be saved once a ride ends, subject to GPS availability.
  • Live location, only while you have explicitly enabled sharing and only while a ride is genuinely active. See Section 4.
  • Diagnostic and crash data, if a crash or error occurs in the app, to help us fix it. This may include device type, OS version, and the app state at the time of the error.

We do not collect your location in the background, and we do not collect it before you start a ride or after sharing is turned off.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to operate RideLink's core features: showing you and your friends who's currently riding, coordinating group rides, sharing live location with people you've explicitly authorized, maintaining your personal ride history and statistics, and operating in-ride safety features (Section 6). We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

4. Live Location Is Temporary By Design

Each live location update automatically expires 30 seconds after it's sent, while a ride is active. There is no history table for it — only your single most recent position is ever stored, and it is deleted the moment a ride ends, is cancelled, or you turn sharing off. Live location is never stored permanently, and precise historical coordinates are never made publicly available.

5. Ride History and Routes

This is separate from live location above: once a ride ends, its distance, duration, and — if recorded — a simplified version of the route you rode are saved to your own ride history, so you can look back on past rides. This history is visible only to you. It is never shown to friends, never made public, and never includes anyone else's position — even for group rides, each rider's own detailed route stays private to them specifically.

6. Who Can See What

Your live location and active-ride status are visible only to friends you've mutually accepted, or to fellow members of the same active group ride — and only while a ride is genuinely active and sharing is on. Setting a ride's visibility to "Only Me" hides it from everyone, including friends. Your profile, ride history, and recorded routes are private by default and only become visible to other riders if you explicitly mark your profile public — and even then, your recorded routes remain private to you.

7. In-Ride Safety Features

RideLink's safety tools — manual SOS and assistive alerts such as "rider may be separated" or "location updates interrupted" — only ever run while you're actively riding, and only report what your device can genuinely observe from GPS and connectivity signals. There is no automatic crash detection, and no background monitoring while you're not riding. These features are best-effort and are not a substitute for your own judgment or attentive riding — see our Terms of Service.

8. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with a small number of service providers to operate RideLink, each receiving only what's needed to run the feature they support:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Mapbox — maps and route rendering.
  • Expo, Apple, and Google — push notification delivery, if you enable notifications.
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics.

None of these providers is authorized to use your data for their own purposes, and RideLink never embeds database administrator credentials in the app itself.

9. Data Retention

Live location data is retained for at most 30 seconds and deleted immediately when a ride ends, is cancelled, or sharing is turned off (Section 4). Ride history, profile, and Garage data are retained until you delete them or delete your account (Section 10).

10. Your Rights and Choices

You can turn live location sharing off at any time from the Map screen, change your ride and profile visibility, manage notification categories, and adjust safety alert preferences from Settings. You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings > Account > Delete Account, without contacting us. Before you confirm, the app shows you exactly what deletion will take with it (your bikes, an active ride if any, and any group ride or event you organize — deleting your account also removes those for every other member, since there's no separate leader-transfer feature). Deletion is immediate and permanent: once confirmed, your account and the data above cannot be recovered, and the same credentials can't be used to sign in again.

11. Children's Privacy

RideLink is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

12. Data Security

We use database-level access controls so that your data — including live location and ride details — is only ever readable by parties this policy describes as authorized to see it (you, your accepted friends, or fellow active group ride members, as applicable), enforced independently of the app itself.

13. Changes to This Policy

Because RideLink is in active beta, this policy may change as the app evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when it does, and material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

14. Contact Us

Account deletion is self-serve in the app (Section 10) and doesn't require contacting us. For any other question about this policy or your data, reach us at sminhas1989@gmail.com.
